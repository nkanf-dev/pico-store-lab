## PICO Store Lab 0.2.0

### 国际区 PICO 账号适配

在头显上的 Lab 登录账号，搜索 **Virtual Desktop**，选择适配版下载并安装。安装后按需打开应用，即可使用国际区 PICO 账号登录。

下载其他使用 PICO 账号组件的应用时，Lab 也会询问是否尝试通用适配。安装未适配版本前，Lab 会提醒你可能无法使用 PICO 账号登录。

适配 profiles 现可独立于 Lab 更新；专用 profile 优先于通用 profile，应用版本不一致时仍可尝试。网站新增 [Virtual Desktop 应用页](https://pico.kanglives.top/apps/virtual-desktop/)、[国际区登录适配指南](https://pico.kanglives.top/guides/international-sign-in/)和[技术原理说明](https://pico.kanglives.top/guides/how-adaptation-works/)。网页只能下载原版 APK；适配版请使用头显客户端。Android Lab 也加入了版本更新公告。

头显端下载 `pico-store-android.apk`。桌面版和 SDK 安装包见下方附件，校验值见 `SHA256SUMS`。

### International PICO sign-in adaptation

On your headset, sign in to Lab, search for **Virtual Desktop**, and choose the adapted download. After installation, open Virtual Desktop normally and sign in with your PICO international account.

For other apps that use PICO account components, Lab can offer a general adaptation when you download them. You choose whether to try it. Lab also warns you before installing an unadapted copy that may be unable to use PICO sign-in.

Compatibility profiles can now be updated separately from the Lab app. App-specific profiles take priority over the general profile, including when an app version differs from the one a profile was built against.

The website now has a [Virtual Desktop page](https://pico.kanglives.top/apps/virtual-desktop/), an [international sign-in guide](https://pico.kanglives.top/guides/international-sign-in/), and a [technical explanation](https://pico.kanglives.top/guides/how-adaptation-works/). Web downloads provide the original APK; use the headset app for adapted installations. Lab also displays release announcements in the Android app.

Download `pico-store-android.apk` for the headset. Desktop downloads and SDK packages are attached below. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
